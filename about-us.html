Our Story

It started with one borrowed oven and a loaf that did not rise.

In 2024, four friends at King Saud University kept staying behind after their last lecture to bake together in a shared kitchen. Word spread, more students joined, and the failed loaves slowly turned into good ones.

Today Crumbs After Class runs weekly sessions for complete beginners and confident bakers alike. The rules have not changed: everyone gets their hands in the dough, nobody bakes alone, and nobody leaves empty-handed.

  • 2024 One oven, four friends
