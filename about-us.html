About Us The best club around!

Who we are, how we started, and the people who teach our sessions.

Our mission

Crumbs After Class gives students a warm place to learn real baking after lectures, take home what they make, and meet people over a shared task.

Our Story

Crumbs After Class began as a small group of students baking together between classes. What started with one loaf of bread grew into a club where members learn new skills each term, from sourdough to laminated pastry.

Today we run weekly sessions led by experienced bakers, and every member leaves with something they made themselves.
