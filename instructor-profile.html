Bio

Reema baked her first loaf in her grandmother's kitchen and has not stopped since. She leads all of our bread sessions, from the free First Loaf to Sourdough from Scratch, and is known for explaining every step twice and for never letting a student leave with a flat loaf.

Outside the club she keeps a sourdough starter that is older than most of her students.
